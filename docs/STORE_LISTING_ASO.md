## English (en-US)

### App title (≤ 30 chars)
```
SuperBiz — POS & Invoices
```

### Short description (≤ 80 chars)
```
Offline-first POS, inventory, debts & ZATCA tax invoices — 100% no internet
```

### Full description
```
SuperBiz — the complete business manager for shops and small businesses in Saudi Arabia. Everything works 100% offline — your data stays on your device, no loading screens.

✔ Fast POS: barcode scanning, quick sale, Bluetooth thermal receipts
✔ Tax invoices: ZATCA phase-1 QR, periodic tax report, full legal XML export
✔ Smart inventory: low-stock alerts, barcode scanning, product archiving, smart analytics
✔ Debts & accounting: receivables/payables, installments, checks, pro PDF statements (50 templates)
✔ Reports: sales & profit, cash flow, periodic tax, charts, XLSX & CSV export
✔ Loyalty & coupons: points and discounts inside the transaction
✔ Security: PIN/biometric lock, amount privacy, full backup & restore
✔ On-device intelligence: 40+ analytics algorithms (forecasting, receivables aging, outliers) with zero internet
✔ Arabic-first: full RTL UI + English, clear numerals everywhere
✔ SuperBiz Pro (optional one-time purchase): KPI board — sales/profit/expenses/receivables vs monthly targets

Core features are free forever. Extra Pro features are one single purchase (no subscription).

Who is it for? Grocery, retail, electronics, services, distributors — any business that needs an organized cashbox and controlled debts without internet.
```
